## Role Requirement → Evidence Matrix

| TELUS Requirement | Evidence | Source |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Evaluate relevance & effectiveness of ads vs. user search queries, keywords, intent** | Nine advertisements scored across three queries on a five-dimension rubric; two ads on the same query scored 9.4 and 3.0, with the gap fully documented | [Case Study 01](../case-studies/01-ad-quality-evaluation.md) |
| **Analyze relationships between search terms, keywords, ad creatives, landing pages, user behavior** | Per-ad-group diagnostic (expected CTR, ad relevance, landing page experience) with specific optimization action attached | [Case Study 02](../case-studies/02-search-campaign-blueprint.md) |
| **Apply Google Ads best practices (campaign structures, keyword strategies, search intent, ad relevance, Quality Score, landing page experience)** | 24 keywords architected across Exact, Phrase, and Broad match; 30 more classified by intent and match type; full four-ad-group architecture built around one stated KPI | [Case Studies 02 & 03](../case-studies/) |
| **Review advertising content following guidelines and quality standards** | Identical rubric applied without exception across all nine evaluated advertisements; every score justified with written evidence | [Case Study 01](../case-studies/01-ad-quality-evaluation.md) |
| **Conduct research to validate ad alignment with user needs** | Four-part intent framework applied to a 30-keyword universe with priority ranking; live search conducted in incognito sessions to minimize personalization bias | [Case Study 03](../case-studies/03-keyword-intent-mapping.md) |
| **Provide clear, actionable feedback for AI-powered advertising systems** | Every evaluation closes with a specific, written recommendation, down to the exact headline revision proposed | [Case Study 01](../case-studies/01-ad-quality-evaluation.md) |
| **Interpret and apply guidelines consistently with high accuracy** | Rubric designed with named, discrete dimensions specifically for reproducibility; calibration-ready structure | [Scoring Rubric](./scoring-rubric.md) |
| **Participate in calibration sessions, consensus discussions, quality reviews** | Framework built on inter-rater reliability principles (Cohen, 1960); scores designed to be challengeable and revisable by a second reviewer | [DJPR9.0 Framework](./djpr9.0-framework.md) |
