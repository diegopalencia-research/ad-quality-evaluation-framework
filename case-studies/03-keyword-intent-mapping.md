# Case Study 03: Keyword Intent Mapping — Online Fitness Coaching (Guatemala)

> **Objective:** Classify a 30-keyword universe by search intent, prioritize by proximity to conversion, and develop budget allocation and negative keyword strategies.

---

## Market Context

The online fitness coaching market in Guatemala is emerging, driven by:
- Increasing smartphone penetration
- Social media fitness content consumption
- Post-pandemic adoption of remote services

**Target Audience:** Urban professionals aged 25–45 seeking personalized fitness guidance, weight loss support, and nutritional planning.

**Competitive Landscape:** International platforms (Beachbody, Noom), regional providers, independent coaches via social media.

---

## Intent Classification Framework

Based on Broder (2002) and extended with the practitioner-defined **Commercial Investigation** category:

| Intent Category | Defining Characteristic | Budget Priority | Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Informational** | Seeking knowledge; not yet evaluating providers | **Low** | Nurture via content/SEO, not paid search |
| **Commercial Investigation** | Comparing providers ahead of a decision | **Medium** | Converts within 2–3 days of first contact |
| **Transactional** | Ready to act (hire, book, buy, sign up) | **High** | Direct revenue generation; primary budget target |
| **Navigational** | Searching for a known brand by name | **Protect** | Brand defense, not conquest |

## Search Volume Funnel Analysis

The share of search volume declines as intent progresses toward conversion:

| Intent Stage | Share of Search Volume | Interpretation |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| Informational | 100% | Broadest reach; lowest conversion readiness |
| Commercial | 72% | Users filtering toward specific providers |
| Transactional | 44% | High-intent minority; highest value per click |
| Navigational | 18% | Smallest volume; brand-loyal audience |

**Key Insight:** The steepest single decline occurs between **Commercial Investigation** and **Transactional** — this is the highest-value inflection point in the funnel. Budget should concentrate where volume drops, not where it peaks.

> *"The highest-value inflection point is not where search volume is largest, but where it drops most sharply."*

## Negative Keyword Strategy

### Irrelevant Services
| Negative Keyword | Rationale |
|------------------|-----------|
| `free fitness coaching` | Service is paid; attracts price-only shoppers |
| `government fitness program` | Private service; public program mismatch |
| `gym equipment` | Service-only business; no product sales |
| `fitness coach certification` | Aspiring coaches, not clients |

### Wrong Intent
| Negative Keyword | Rationale |
|------------------|-----------|
| `fitness coach jobs` | Employment seekers, not buyers |
| `become a personal trainer` | Career changers, not service seekers |
| `fitness coaching course` | Educational intent, not transactional |

### Geographic Mismatch
| Negative Keyword | Rationale |
|------------------|-----------|
| `fitness coach USA` | Service limited to Guatemala/time zones |
| `online trainer Europe` | Geographic/service mismatch |
| `personal trainer Mexico City` | Wrong metro market |
