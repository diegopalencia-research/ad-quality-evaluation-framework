## 🎯 The Three Case Studies

| # | Study | Focus | Key Output |
|---|-------|-------|------------|
| **01** | [Ad Quality Evaluation](./case-studies/01-ad-quality-evaluation.md) | Live ad scoring across 3 real search queries | 9 ads scored on 5 dimensions; modifier sensitivity pattern identified |
| **02** | [Search Campaign Blueprint](./case-studies/02-search-campaign-blueprint.md) | Full account architecture for LexGuatemala | 4 ad groups, 24 keywords, 8 RSAs, 3-phase bidding strategy |
| **03** | [Keyword Intent Mapping](./case-studies/03-keyword-intent-mapping.md) | 30-keyword universe for fitness coaching market | Intent-classified keyword set with budget allocation framework |

### Cross-Cutting Findings

1. **Query Modifier Sensitivity**: Ads addressing modifiers in headlines scored **3.2× higher** on relevance than generic copy.
2. **Intent-Alignment Threshold**: Intent Match below 6.0 universally caps overall quality; above 8.0 enables top-tier scores even with moderate performance elsewhere.
3. **Landing Page Ceiling**: No ad scored more than 1.0 point above its Landing Page Experience score; LPX functions as a hard quality ceiling.

## 🧠 The DJPR9.0 Framework

**Decision Journey Process Reduction 9.0** is an applied Decision Intelligence methodology for transforming information into structured evaluative decisions.

**Core Philosophy:** Information → Insight → Decision → Value

**Four-Phase Cycle:**
| Phase | Action | Output |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Observe** | Gather query, ad, landing page, competitive context | Comprehensive data without premature judgment |
| **Analyze** | Apply structured criteria; identify patterns; measure relevance | Structured evidence |
| **Interpret** | Generate insights; prioritize improvements; reduce uncertainty | Actionable diagnosis |
| **Recommend** | Produce scored evaluation with documented reasoning | Calibratable feedback for AI systems |

## 📐 The Five-Dimension Scoring Rubric

Every evaluated advertisement is scored independently across five dimensions (1–10 scale), consistent with Google's published Quality Score methodology:

| Dimension | What It Measures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Keyword Alignment** | Correspondence between query terms and ad copy |
| **Intent Match** | Whether the ad addresses the underlying reason for the search |
| **Ad Relevance** | Clarity, specificity, and quality of the ad copy itself |
| **Landing Page Fit** | Correspondence between the ad's promise and the page it links to |
| **Trust Signals** | Credibility markers (social proof, domain authority, specificity) |

[→ View the complete rubric with scoring anchors](./appendix/scoring-rubric.md)
