# Paper Summary

**Title:** Evaluating Search Advertisement Quality Through an Applied Decision Intelligence Framework

**Author:** Diego Palencia Robles

## Three Case Studies
1. **Ad Quality Evaluation** — 9 live ads scored across 3 queries using a 5-dimension rubric
2. **Campaign Blueprint** — Full Google Ads architecture for LexGuatemala (4 ad groups, 24 keywords)
3. **Keyword Intent Mapping** — 30 keywords classified by intent with budget allocation framework

## Key Finding
Ads that address query modifiers directly in headlines score **3.2× higher** on relevance than generic copy.

## Framework
DJPR9.0 — Observe → Analyze → Interpret → Recommend
